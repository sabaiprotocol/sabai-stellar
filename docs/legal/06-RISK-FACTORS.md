# Risk Factors

*Specimen — reference implementation. Delivered with every subscription; the
company and its asset are illustrative.*

**The Asset.** The Company owns a single property. Income and value depend
entirely on that one asset: its occupancy, condition, management, insurance
and market. There is no portfolio effect. Valuations are periodic third-party
appraisals, and the realizable price on a sale may differ from any appraisal.

**The Asset's jurisdiction.** The property, its title regime, its taxes and
its courts sit in the Asset's own jurisdiction. Rental income is distributed
after local withholding and charges; changes in local law, tax rates,
title or lease terms directly change holder returns.

**Restricted securities.** Units are membership interests offered privately
under Regulation S and are not registered under the U.S. Securities Act or
any other law. They may never be offered or sold to U.S. persons, and every
transfer settles only between verified non-U.S. persons through the
register's eligibility gate. A holder who loses eligibility keeps their units
but cannot move them while ineligible.

**Liquidity.** The only organized venue is the platform's internal market,
inside an operator-published price band; the standing buyback, where funded,
repurchases below the primary price. A holder may be unable to exit at a
chosen time or price, and no listing on any exchange is promised.

**Concentration and member caps.** The operating agreement enforces a 9.99%
per-person cap and a member-count limit that preserves the Company's U.S.
partnership tax classification. A subscription or transfer that would breach
a cap is refused, which can constrain both entry and exit.

**Tax.** The Company is a U.S. partnership for tax purposes; each member
receives an annual schedule of allocated results and is responsible for tax
in their own jurisdiction. Tax law or classification changes — including
loss of partnership status or a change in income source — could increase the
tax borne by the Company or its members.

**Technology.** Ownership is recorded on smart contracts on a public
network. Contract defects, network failures, or loss of a holder's own keys
can impair access to units or distributions. Mitigations are structural, not
absolute: independent contract audits before any live deployment, a
multi-signature administration with bounded roles, and a forced-transfer
procedure for court orders, probate and verified key loss.

**Operations.** Holders rely on the Manager, the platform operator, the
property manager, the verification provider and the tax provider performing
their roles. Failure, replacement, or insolvency of any of them can
interrupt distributions or reporting even where the Asset performs.

**No assurance.** Distributions are neither fixed nor guaranteed; past or
projected figures are not a promise of future results, and the value of
units can fall to zero.
