# Articles of Organization — Asset DAO LLC

*Specimen — reference implementation. The company and its asset are
illustrative; the smart contract identifiers are the live testnet deployment
of this repository.*

Prepared for filing with the Wyoming Secretary of State under the Wyoming
Limited Liability Company Act and the Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous
Organization Supplement, W.S. 17-31-101 et seq.

**Article I — Name.** The name of the limited liability company is
**Asset DAO LLC**. The company is a decentralized autonomous organization
within the meaning of the Supplement, and its name includes the designation
required by W.S. 17-31-106.

**Article II — Statutory notice.** NOTICE OF RESTRICTIONS ON DUTIES AND
TRANSFERS: The rights of members in a decentralized autonomous organization
may differ materially from the rights of members in other limited liability
companies. The Wyoming Decentralized Autonomous Organization Supplement,
underlying smart contracts, articles of organization and operating agreement,
if applicable, of a decentralized autonomous organization may define, reduce
or eliminate fiduciary duties and may restrict transfer of ownership
interests, withdrawal or resignation from the decentralized autonomous
organization, return of capital contributions and dissolution of the
decentralized autonomous organization.

**Article III — Management.** The company is a member-managed decentralized
autonomous organization. Membership interests are represented by digital
units recorded on the smart contracts identified in Article IV, and the
register maintained by those smart contracts is the definitive record of
membership, as provided in the company's operating agreement.

**Article V — Purpose.** The company's sole purpose is to acquire, own,
operate and dispose of a single real estate asset, "Sabai Lagoon Residence
No. 1" — directly, or through a registered leasehold or a wholly-owned local
title-holding entity where the law of the asset's situs requires one —
together with activities incidental to that purpose. The company shall own
no other asset of substance.

**Article VI — Registered agent and office.** The company's registered agent
is a commercial registered agent in the State of Wyoming, at the agent's
registered office stated in the filing.

**Article VII — Principal office.** The principal office of the company is
the office of its manager stated in the filing.

**Article VIII — Duration and dissolution.** The company's duration is
perpetual, subject to dissolution as provided by the Supplement and the
operating agreement, including dissolution upon the sale of the asset and
distribution of proceeds.

**Organizer.** These articles are executed by the organizer named in the
filing.
