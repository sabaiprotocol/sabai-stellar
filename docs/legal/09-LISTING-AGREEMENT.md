# Tokenization & Listing Agreement

*Specimen — reference implementation. Both parties are illustrative. Used in
the marketplace model, where the asset belongs to a third-party asset holder
rather than to the platform's own group.*

Between **Panama Tokenization Platform Inc.** (the **"Operator"**) and the
asset holder identified in the schedule (the **"Asset Holder"**).

## 1. The engagement

The Asset Holder engages the Operator to tokenize the asset identified in
the schedule and to list the resulting membership tokens on the Operator's
platform. Tokenization follows the platform's standard structure: a
per-asset company formed for the sole purpose of holding the asset, a fixed
unit supply on the platform's register contracts, and the constitutional
bundle anchored on-chain.

## 2. Sequence

1. The per-asset company is formed in the jurisdiction agreed in the
   schedule; the Asset Holder (or its nominee) is its initial Sponsor
   Member.
2. The contract set is deployed; the unit supply is issued into treasury
   custody; the contract identifiers are written into the company's
   constitutional filings.
3. Title to the asset is transferred to (or contracted to be acquired by)
   the per-asset company, supported by a title report and an independent
   valuation.
4. The constitutional bundle is anchored via the register's terms function,
   and the primary offering opens on the platform.

## 3. Asset Holder representations

The Asset Holder represents that: it has, and will convey to the per-asset
company, good title to the asset free of undisclosed encumbrances; the
independent valuation and title report delivered under this agreement are
the ones on which the offering documents rely; and all information it
supplies for the offering is accurate and not misleading.

## 4. Asset Holder obligations

Fund the per-asset company's distributions from the asset's net income,
after taxes and charges of the asset's jurisdiction; maintain property
management and insurance; deliver annual accounts of the per-asset company;
and notify the Operator without delay of any event affecting title, value,
or income.

## 5. Operator obligations

Operate the technology and the markets; run the verification process through
the licensed provider; maintain the four registers and the reporting cycle
under the Operating & Compliance Policy; and administer the platform roles
on the contracts under that policy's key-custody rules. The Operator does
not guarantee subscription volume and does not underwrite the offering.

## 6. Fees

A structuring fee on setup, a percentage of the primary offering proceeds,
and the platform's secondary market commission, each as set out in the
schedule; distribution processing at cost.

## 7. Responsibility split

As between the parties: the asset, its title, its performance and the
funding of distributions are the Asset Holder's responsibility; the
technology, the registers, the verification process and market operation are
the Operator's. Neither party makes the other's representations to
investors, and the offering documents state which party stands behind which
statement.

## 8. Delisting and wind-down

On expiry or termination, no new offering is made. Existing tokens remain
outstanding and serviced: the registers, distributions and the internal
market continue for so long as units are outstanding, or until the per-asset
company is dissolved and proceeds distributed under its operating agreement.
The Asset Holder's funding and information obligations survive for the same
period.

## 9. Governing law and disputes

The law and forum stated in the schedule; absent a choice, the laws of the
Republic of Panama and arbitration in English at the seat published by the
Operator.
