# Platform Terms of Service

*Specimen — reference implementation. The operator is illustrative.*

The platform is operated by **Panama Tokenization Platform Inc.** (the
**"Operator"**). These terms govern access to the platform and its markets.

## 1. What the platform is

The platform provides access to membership tokens issued by separate
per-asset companies. Each token is issued by its own company under its own
constitutional documents, anchored on the register; **the Operator is not
the issuer** of any asset token, and is not a broker, dealer, investment
adviser, or custodian of fiat money. The Operator operates the technology:
the markets, the verification process, the registers and the reporting.

## 2. Access and verification

An account is opened only after identity verification by the platform's
licensed verification provider, including screening against sanctions lists
and confirmation of non-U.S.-person status. Only the eligibility decision is
written to the on-chain registry; verification data itself never leaves the
provider and is never written to any public ledger. The Operator may decline
or terminate access where verification cannot be completed or maintained.

## 3. Eligibility

The platform serves persons who are not U.S. persons under Regulation S and
who are not residents of jurisdictions on the platform's restricted list
published in the application. Eligibility is enforced on-chain: units settle
only to and from verified addresses, on both sides of every movement.

## 4. Markets

**Primary.** Subscriptions are made under each company's subscription
agreement at the published price, with proceeds escrowed until that
company's Final Closing.

**Secondary.** The internal peer-to-peer market opens on completion of an
asset's primary sale. Listings execute within the published price band;
settlement is atomic against escrowed units; the platform charges the
commission published per market, deducted at fill.

**Buyback.** Where an issuer funds a standing buyback, holders may sell back
at the published discount to the primary price.

## 5. Settlement

Markets settle in the digital settlement assets stated in the application.
The Operator does not accept or hold fiat currency; where fiat access is
offered it is provided by named third-party anchors under their own terms.

## 6. Suspension

The Operator may halt markets, and the verification provider may suspend or
revoke a holder's eligibility, in each case as described in the Operating &
Compliance Policy. Suspension of a holder or a halt of the market does not
cancel distributions already earned, which remain claimable per the issuing
company's operating agreement.

## 7. Holder obligations

One person — one verified identity; addresses are linked to it. Holders keep
their credentials and keys safe, use the platform for their own account
only, and comply with the tax obligations of their own residence. Market
manipulation, attempts to transfer to unverified addresses, and use of the
platform to solicit U.S. persons are prohibited.

## 8. Fees

Fees are published in the application per market and per service and are
deducted at execution. Changes apply prospectively.

## 9. Liability

The Operator is responsible for operating the platform with due skill. The
Operator is not liable for the performance of any asset, the decisions of
any issuing company, network congestion or failure of the public ledger, or
loss caused by a holder's own keys or credentials. Nothing excludes
liability that cannot be excluded by law.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Panama. Disputes are
resolved by arbitration at the seat and under the rules published in the
