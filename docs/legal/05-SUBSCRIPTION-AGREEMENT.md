# Membership Token Subscription Agreement

*Specimen — reference implementation. The company and its asset are
illustrative.*

Between **Asset DAO LLC**, a Wyoming decentralized autonomous organization
limited liability company (the **"Company"**), and the subscriber identified
by the verified platform account executing this agreement (the
**"Subscriber"**).

## 1. Subscription

The Subscriber irrevocably subscribes for the number of units of the Company
confirmed at checkout, at the published price per unit, one unit represented
by one token on the Company's membership register. Payment is made in the
settlement asset stated at checkout to the escrow account designated by the
Company.

## 2. Escrow and closing

Subscription proceeds are held in escrow until Final Closing under the
operating agreement. At Final Closing the subscribed units are delivered on
the register to the Subscriber's verified address, and economic and voting
rights activate. If the funding target is not reached by the longstop date
stated at checkout, the subscription terminates and contributions are
mandatorily returned in full.

## 3. Eligibility and Regulation S

The Subscriber represents and warrants, at signing and at delivery, that the
Subscriber:

1. is **not a U.S. person** as defined in Regulation S under the U.S.
   Securities Act of 1933, is not acquiring for the account or benefit of a
   U.S. person, and is outside the United States, this transaction being an
   offshore transaction under Regulation S;
2. has completed the platform's verification, and every address to receive
   units is the Subscriber's own verified address;
3. acquires for the Subscriber's own account, not with a view to
   distribution, and will resell only in transactions permitted by the
   operating agreement — through the register's eligibility gate, to verified
   non-U.S. persons;
4. understands the units are not registered under the U.S. Securities Act or
   any other securities law, and no public market exists or is promised;
5. is not a sanctioned person and is not acting for one, and is not a
   politically exposed person, or has disclosed that status in verification;
6. complies with the private placement conditions of the Subscriber's own
   jurisdiction.

## 4. Joinder

Upon acceptance of this subscription and delivery of units, the Subscriber
is admitted as a member of the Company and becomes a party to and bound by
the operating agreement, without further act.

## 5. Acknowledgments

The Subscriber acknowledges having received and read the constitutional
bundle — the articles of organization, the operating agreement, this
agreement and the risk factors — and that the bundle's sha256 hash and
location are recorded on the membership register through its terms function;
the version so anchored is the version that governs. The Subscriber
acknowledges the risk factors in full, including that distributions depend
on the Asset's performance, that transfers are restricted, and that the
concentration and member caps of the operating agreement may cause a
subscription or transfer to be refused.

## 6. Protective consents before Final Closing

Before Final Closing, the Subscriber's consent rights are limited to the
protective matters listed in the operating agreement: amendment of this
agreement or of the escrow terms, extension of the longstop date, and any
use of escrowed funds other than the Asset acquisition or their return.

## 7. Data

Verification data is processed by the platform's licensed verification
provider under the platform's terms of service; it is not written to any
public ledger. Only the eligibility decision reaches the register.

## 8. Governing law and disputes

This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. Disputes are
resolved by binding arbitration at the seat and under the rules stated in
the operating procedure, in English.
